## What I've built

**[HostelMate](https://github.com/nivo-in/hostelmate)** · [live demo](https://hostelmate-vert.vercel.app)
One platform for college hostels: students, wardens and parents get attendance, leave, complaints, mess, fees and visitors in one place, instead of paper registers and WhatsApp groups. Attendance can't be marked for a friend: a rotating HMAC-signed QR code checked against a 100 m GPS fence. Face check runs in the browser, complaints are triaged by an LLM, and fees go through Razorpay with every signature verified. Sole engineer (1,124 commits), with 260+ Jest and 59 Playwright tests on every commit.
`Next.js` `Express` `Supabase` `Redis` `Socket.io` `Turborepo`

**[AI Meeting Intelligence](https://github.com/kartikeyagrawal2007/ai-meeting-intelligence)**
Turns meeting audio into action items, decisions and open questions, each linked to the exact quote and speaker. 6.67% word error rate on multi-speaker audio, where Whisper Large-v3 scored 40%.
`Python` `FastAPI` `AssemblyAI` `Groq`

**[FoodBridge](https://github.com/kartikeyagrawal2007/FoodBridge)** · [demo video](https://youtu.be/XnkwVpmIzhA)
A marketplace where restaurants list surplus food and NGOs claim it before it expires; claims are atomic, so two NGOs can never take the same food. Harvard CS50x final project.
`Python` `Flask` `SQLite`

**[AshCam](https://github.com/kartikeyagrawal2007/ashcam)**
A macOS virtual camera: snap your fingers and you turn to dust on a video call, snap again to come back. Works in Meet, Zoom, FaceTime and WhatsApp.
`Python` `OpenCV` `MediaPipe` `ONNX Runtime`

## Research

**Do small models talk themselves out of right answers?** · *Accepted at ICTCS 2026*
A trace-level study of reasoning in small language models (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B, Qwen2.5-3B, Llama-3.2-3B). Of 11 traces that looked like a model abandoning a right answer, only 1 held up once the probe's own effect was separated out. Co-author.

**[Gearbox](https://github.com/kartikeyagrawal2007/gearbox)** · *paper in progress*
An automatic transmission for AI agents: hand subtasks to cheaper models and accept an answer only once it passes a real test. 95.7% on HumanEval+ at about a third of the compute of always using the 27B model.

**Amazon ML Challenge 2026** · *top 5% (rank ~1,800 of 35,000)*
Matching one business across 12.5M messy records in several Indian scripts: candidate search, a LightGBM matcher and one-to-one matching.
